Doodle Stories – Free AI Bedtime Story Generator for Kids

Turn your child's drawing into a magical personalized bedtime story with AI. Free kids storytelling app for ages 2-13. No account required.

For Parents

Looking for a fun bedtime story app for your child? Doodle Stories turns any drawing into a personalized AI story read aloud in seconds. Perfect for winding down at night. Free, safe, no ads, no accounts.

For Teachers

Bring creative writing to life in your classroom. Students draw anything and Doodle Stories instantly generates a personalized story. Perfect for art integration, literacy lessons, and creative writing for kids ages 2-13.

For Kids

Draw your favourite things and watch them come alive in a story! Upload your drawing or draw right in the app and hear your very own magical story read aloud just for you.

How It Works

  1. Upload a photo of your drawing or draw in the app
  2. Choose your age group — Tiny Tots, Little Readers, Story Lovers, or Big Kids
  3. Tap Make My Story and AI creates your personalized story
  4. Listen to your story read aloud or save it to the Bedtime Story Library
  5. Share as an Instagram carousel with family and friends
